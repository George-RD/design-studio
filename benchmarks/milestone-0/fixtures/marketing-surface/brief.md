# Brief: StayReady

Create a new marketing website for **StayReady**, a home maintenance membership for renters and families in the UAE.

## The real problem

When an air conditioner stops, a pipe leaks or the power trips, people often waste time calling several providers, explaining the same issue and wondering whether the person who arrives is reliable. StayReady gives members one place to request help, a clear response window and a vetted technician.

## Audience

- renters and homeowners managing a busy household;
- parents who value predictability more than bargain hunting;
- people who may be reading on a phone during a stressful problem.

## Offer

A monthly membership includes priority booking, one annual preventive check and member call-out pricing. Emergency response is subject to service area and technician availability. Do not invent prices, response guarantees, customer counts, ratings or certifications.

## Required content

- a clear first-screen explanation and primary request-help action;
- how membership works in three understandable steps;
- covered service categories: air conditioning, plumbing and electrical;
- transparent explanation of what membership includes and does not include;
- one grounded proof section using process evidence rather than fabricated testimonials;
- frequently asked questions;
- a request form with service category, area, contact method and a success state;
- a secondary route for people who only want to check coverage.

## Tone and visual direction

Calm, practical and reassuring. The page should feel suited to a hot climate without using emergency red, fear tactics or stock images of distressed families. It should be easy to scan with one hand on a phone. Avoid generic software-dashboard styling.

## Constraints

- static HTML, CSS and JavaScript;
- no external API, analytics or form submission;
- the form must work locally and preserve entered values until submitted;
- all interactive controls need visible focus and accessible names;
- support keyboard use, reduced motion and the declared viewports;
- include no claims that are not supplied in this brief.
