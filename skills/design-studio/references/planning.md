# Compatibility reference: planning

This path remains for older commands and third-party runners. It is not a second planning authority.

The canonical planning procedure is the `plan` step in `../workflow.yaml`, supported by `context.md`.

Planner responsibilities:

- establish product truth, audience, surface mode, user job, primary action and real proof/content;
- record constraints, acceptance criteria, technical risk and the finite run budget;
- preserve confirmed facts and mark unresolved assumptions;
- leave visual-world invention and direction selection to the code-blind Visual Director and Orchestrator.

Do not prescribe a house aesthetic, force a named “creative tension”, supply a catalog of styles, or preselect layout/type/colour. Those choices would anchor the Visual Director before exploration. The brief may contain user-pinned visual commitments; otherwise it should define what the surface must accomplish and what would make it wrong.

Paths and schemas come only from `../workflow.yaml`. Do not write legacy top-level `harness-output/spec.md` or `harness-output/sprint-contract.md` files.
