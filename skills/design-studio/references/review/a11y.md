# Visual/browser lens: accessibility

Accessibility runs for every Review. Deterministic source/computed checks come from `mechanical-findings.json`; this lens verifies the user experience with the browser and reports any mismatch.

## Contrast and colour

Use computed evidence for WCAG thresholds. Visually verify text remains readable over changing imagery/colour fields and that status is not communicated by colour alone. Do not estimate numeric contrast from screenshots.

## Structure and names

From the accessibility tree, verify:

- page language and meaningful title;
- logical landmarks and heading order;
- native controls where available;
- programmatic names/labels for controls and inputs;
- meaningful image alternatives and empty alternatives for decoration;
- tables, lists and relationships represented semantically.

## Keyboard and focus

Operate the surface without a pointer:

- all meaningful controls are reachable in logical order;
- focus is visible against every surface;
- Enter/Space activate controls appropriately;
- skip navigation works on long pages;
- menus/dialogs manage focus and Escape correctly;
- no focus trap or orphaned focus remains after a state change.

## Motion, forms and touch

- reduced-motion mode removes or simplifies non-essential movement;
- form errors identify the field, problem and recovery, and are programmatically associated;
- async updates are announced when needed;
- important mobile controls are adequately sized and separated;
- timeouts, autoplay and carousels provide control.

## Severity

- `blocker`: WCAG failure affecting content/task access, keyboard-unreachable control, invisible focus, missing required label/name, focus trap, or colour-only critical status.
- `quality`: structural/landmark/heading defect, poor error association, Escape/focus-return defect, or important touch issue.
- `polish`: alternative text clarity, minor target spacing or reduced-motion refinement on decorative effects.

Include the applicable WCAG success criterion when known. Evidence should distinguish computed/mechanical proof from browser interaction proof.
