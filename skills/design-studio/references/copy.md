# Customer-facing copy boundary

## Purpose

Coordinate copy that must fit a designed surface while keeping offer, positioning, persuasion strategy and authoritative commercial copy outside the Design Studio method kernel.

## Triggers

Load when a surface adds or materially rewrites customer-facing copy, when an approved external copy artifact is supplied, when the context model emits the `composition-artifacts` evidence signal, or when layout work exposes a wording constraint that affects comprehension/action.

## Required context

For standalone Design Studio work, authority order is current user instruction; confirmed facts/claim boundaries in `PRODUCT.md`; durable language rules in `COPY.md` when present; current surface brief; incumbent copy as baseline.

When composition evidence exists, load `../composition-contract.json` and `composition-contract.md` before deciding which artifact is authoritative. Resolve by declared role/scope and approval provenance, not basename, prompt order or modification time. Confirmed product truth always outranks conflicting copy. Never weaken a factual, legal or commercial qualification for brevity.

## Outputs and handoff

For local surface wording, keep the point early, one main idea per sentence, concrete nouns/verbs, consistent terminology and a clear next action. Freeze incumbent copy before replacement and compare audience recognition, category/mechanism clarity, specificity, action clarity, claim discipline and voice. Strategic offer or positioning changes hand back to the external copy authority or user.

When consuming a compatible Growth Arsenal export, preserve its approved claims, qualifications, terminology and strategic copy decisions as the `offer-copy` role. Design Studio may adapt layout or propose local clarity changes, but it does not silently replace approved strategy. If a visual constraint cannot coexist with authoritative copy, surface the cross-domain trade-off instead of choosing a winner by prompt order.

## Authority boundary

Growth Arsenal owns offer, positioning, persuasion strategy and authoritative commercial copy outside the Design Studio method kernel. Design Studio consumes approved composition artifacts; it does not invoke, copy or reimplement Growth Arsenal methods. This leaf owns only design-side copy coordination and safe local wording within confirmed facts.

When Growth Arsenal is absent, Design Studio remains fully usable with user/repository copy and these local coordination rules. That fallback does not claim offer-strategy or persuasion-method authority.

## Failure behavior

When proof is missing, retain the qualification or mark the claim unresolved. Do not invent customers, prices, benchmarks, capabilities or testimonials. If a requested change materially alters offer/positioning rather than surface clarity, stop that copy change and request/consume the appropriate external artifact.

If multiple candidate artifacts still claim the same composition role after role/scope/provenance resolution, mark the dependent decision unresolved. If product truth changes underneath approved copy, mark the affected copy stale and return it to its authority before final acceptance.

## Evaluation hooks

Changed copy must pass position, read-aloud, specificity, action and trust checks. Public landing-page copy should also survive a fast skimmer, a right-fit sceptic and a wrong-fit reader. Shorter text or lower reading grade never wins by itself.

For composed work, also confirm that the final surface uses the authoritative project-scoped offer/copy artifact and that no internal Growth Arsenal report asset or same-named file was mistaken for project authority.
